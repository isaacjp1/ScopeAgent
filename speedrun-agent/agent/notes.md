WRENFIELD GUIDE (seen on the scored clinics, Wrenfield 4.3; trust what the pages show if this differs)
- Everything is reached by URL. Tables are shown as "## header | ..." then one row per line, with the row's links after "->".
- Patients: /patients?lastName=X (PHONETIC: "Wells" also returns Wallace..., sorted by last then first name, paged) or /patients?dob=YYYY-MM-DD (best: a few rows). There is no first-name filter. Columns: Patient "Last, First" | MRN | DOB | Sex | Flags (Restricted, Merged, Test record, Deleted).
- Chart /patients/<id> = Demographics (Contact: phone, email, address; Care team: primary provider, home location). Tabs: /insurance (primary insurance payer/plan/member ID or "No insurance on file", Coverage table, Authorizations, Claims not yet filed), /problems, /allergies, /notes (chart notes, phone calls, messages with author, date-time, text), /immunizations (Vaccine | Date given | Lot | Site | Given by | Recorded as), /files (documents: Title | Type | Received -> /patients/<id>/files/<doc>), /orders, /appointments (Date and time | Type | Provider | Location | Status; recurring series link /patients/<id>/recurring/<id> listing every date with its status), /encounters, /scheduling, /claims (claims list -> /claims/<id>), /activity, /flowsheet. Invoices: /billing/accounts/<patient id> (Invoice number | Service date | ... -> /billing/invoices/<id>).
- A merged duplicate chart's link opens the surviving chart ("The record with MRN ... was merged into this record"); what was filed under the duplicate shows in the survivor.
- Restricted charts ask for a reason before opening: do not open them; skip unless the answer is elsewhere.
- Phone digits in page TEXT are scrambled by a font: for phone questions use patient_contact (reads the screen).
- Documents: /patients/<id>/files lists them; open a row's link with read_document (PDF text). Many lab reports and immunization records are scans with no text: use look_at_page on the document page to read the image. Global list: /documents?kind=lab_report|referral_letter|immunization_record|statement|eligibility_response|eob|court_order|medicare_questionnaire&from=YYYY-MM-DD&to=YYYY-MM-DD (patient names + links; patient= filter does not work).
- Schedule: /schedule/appointments?start=YYYY-MM-DD&end=YYYY-MM-DD&provider=<id>&location=<id>&status=<1 Scheduled|2 Completed|3 Cancelled|4 Late cancel|5 No-show>&type=<visit type> (start/end, NOT from/to; ids are in the form's select options; rows marked Recurring link the series). Provider day: /schedule/providers/<id>?date=YYYY-MM-DD ("<Specialty> · <weekday, date>", location, "Times in CST", "Working 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM, ...", rows incl. "Open slot"). Day view /schedule?date=D&location=<id> (one location). Slot finder /schedule/slot-finder.
- Billing: /billing lists invoices (its URL filters number=/patient= are IGNORED: use search_form("/billing", {"number": "<invoice>"}), or patient -> /billing/accounts/<id> -> invoice). Invoice page /billing/invoices/<id>: Total, Status (Open, Partly paid, Paid, Void), Ledger (charges, payments and adjustments) ending in "Amount owed". Balances can change during the shift: re-open, don't trust an old read.
- Claims: /claims/<id>: Details (patient, rendering provider, location, payer, plan, member ID, service date), Service lines (procedure, modifier, units, charge), Submissions (sent, type, result, result date), Remittances (trace · date · Payment or "Reversal of <trace>"; keyed lines with Adjustments like CO-45 $47.60 and Remark codes, or "Received on paper ... on the scanned document": the paper remittance is SCANNED INTO THE PATIENT'S DOCUMENTS (/patients/<id>/files, received about that date): look_at_page on that document to read its lines), Authorizations, Account notes. Payer contract: /claims/payers/<id> ("Claims accepted within N days", "Appeals accepted within N days from the remittance date", fee schedule). Payer profile: /payers/<id> (Claim filing table: Effective | Original | Secondary | Corrected; prior auth). /payers lists payers.
- Vaccines: /vaccines (lots: Lot number | Vaccine | Location | Received | Expires | Doses -> /vaccines/lots/<id>), /vaccines/storage, /vaccines/notes (search the notes of all charts). Their URL filters (?lot=, ?words=) are IGNORED: search_form("/vaccines", {"lot": "<LOT>"}) and search_form("/vaccines/notes", {"words": "<LOT>"}).
- Top menu: / (dashboard, shows today's date), /patients, /schedule, /inbox, /encounters, /billing, /payers, /claims, /documents, /vaccines, /staff, /audit-log, /settings. The clinic calendar (closures, office holidays): /settings?year=YYYY&location=<location id> (entries for all locations or one; only Active entries count). An appointment's own page /patients/<id>/appointments/<appt id> has its Booking date and History (moves of the start time).
- Locations (KS: Central time, CO: Mountain time) are in the schedule's location select. Times on pages carry their zone (CST/CDT/MST/MDT).
- NOT pages: /inventory, /lots, /immunizations?lot=, /claims/remittances/<id>/document.
